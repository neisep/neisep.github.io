**MdNotes** is a small, local, Obsidian-style notes app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Notes are plain `.md` files in a folder of your choice. Nothing leaves your machine, and there is no embedded browser.

- Edit, split and reading views with a live, natively rendered preview
- Mermaid diagrams drawn natively: flowchart, sequence, class, state, ER, gantt, pie, mindmap and more
- `[[Wikilinks]]`, quick switcher, accent-insensitive full-text search and vault-wide search and replace
- System, light and dark themes

Built with .NET 10, Avalonia 12 and Markdig.
