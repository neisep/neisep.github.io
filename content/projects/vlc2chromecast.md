**vlc2chromecast** is a lightweight desktop application that casts any local video file to a Chromecast on your network. Point it at your Chromecast, pick a video and hit play.

Under the hood it launches [VLC](https://www.videolan.org/) in headless mode with Chromecast streaming arguments. There is no complex setup and no command line needed.

- Drag and drop, or browse to a video file
- Pause, resume, stop and seek via a clickable progress bar
- Real-time position, duration and play state
- Runs on Windows and Linux
